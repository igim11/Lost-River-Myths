Tala bringing rice to the Earth

After the Wind God Angin flooded the world with heavy rain, Sinukuan sent down his grandson Tala. He came bearing wisdom, showing people how to tame these water-filled plains with rice. Little did he know, this would bring about the beginning of the Second War of the Gods.
